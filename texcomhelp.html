TexCom Help

TexCom helps you talk by typing or by choosing ready-made phrases, and it speaks them aloud.

You're using TexCom in a web browser. Everything here works except the features marked apps only, which need the TexCom app for iPhone, iPad or Mac.

Speaking

  • Type in the yellow box, then press Speak.
  • Tap a phrase to add it to the box, then press Speak. You can add several phrases, or type more around them.
  • Some phrases are set to speak straight away when you tap them ("Instant message", see Editing).
  • The text box is also the search. As you type, the list shows phrases containing the words since your last full stop, comma, question mark (and so on). Tap one and it replaces those words. For example, type I'm tired. tea and tap "It's not my cup of tea." A Matching line shows while the list is filtered; tap its ✕ to see every phrase again.
  • Shortcuts: type a phrase's shortcut followed by / and it becomes the whole phrase. For example, if "Hello" has the shortcut h, typing h/ gives "Hello".
  • A phrase containing [***] puts in your name (Settings > Your name).

Phrases and categories

  • Phrases are grouped into categories. Tap a category to see its phrases; All shows every phrase.
  • On a phone held upright, tap Categories above the phrases to choose one.
  • Favourites holds the phrases you use most. Add one with the ♡ button, or tick Favourites when editing a phrase.
  • Swipe (or scroll) the lists to see more.
  • On a larger screen, drag the bar between the two columns to make either wider.

Adding and changing phrases

  • Press ✎ Edit. Each phrase and category gets a ✎ button, and "+ Add phrase" / "+ Add category" appear at the top of the lists.
  • In the editor you can change the words, the symbol (type or paste an emoji), a shortcut, and which categories it's in.
  • Instant message: the phrase speaks as soon as you tap it.
  • Delete removes it (you'll be asked to confirm).
  • In Edit mode, drag the ⠿ handle to reorder phrases or categories.
  • Press ✎ Edit again when you've finished.

Talking with someone

Face to face

  • Press 🎙 and TexCom listens to the person you're talking with. What they say appears at the top of the screen, and TexCom can suggest replies. apps only
  • Settings > Smart AI > Start listening when TexCom opens turns the microphone on each time you start TexCom.
  • With a keyboard connected, the other person can press the ` key (next to 1 or Z), type, and press Enter. Their words appear at the top of the screen. Esc cancels.

On their own phone or computer, anywhere

  • Settings > Conversation > Start a conversation… gives you a link and a short code. Share the link (in a message or email), or let them scan the QR code.
  • They can use any phone or computer (they just open the link), or their own TexCom (Join a conversation…, then paste the link or type the code).
  • When they try to join, TexCom asks you: Allow or Decline. Nobody joins without your say-so. Each conversation has a new link.
  • What you say appears very large on their screen. What they type appears in your chat history.
  • The bar above the chat shows who you're talking with. Press End to finish.
  • Read partner's messages aloud (Settings > Conversation) speaks their messages to you.
  • Both of you need an internet connection.

Chat history

  • Settings > Smart AI > Show chat history shows the conversation so far, under the categories (or above the phrases on a phone held upright). It always shows during a conversation over the internet.
  • Drag the bar above it to make it bigger or smaller.

Smart AI apps only

These run privately on your device (Apple Intelligence), so nothing you say is sent anywhere. They need a recent iPhone, iPad or Mac.

  • Suggested replies: after you or your partner speak, up to three replies appear at the top of the phrase list, marked 🧠. Tap one to use it. Type ^ to ask for new suggestions.
  • Next-word predictions: as you type, likely next words appear as buttons. Tap one to add it.
  • Smart Expand: type a short note and end it with @ (for example need drink@) and TexCom writes it out as a full sentence. Switch it on in Settings > Smart AI.
  • AI persona (Settings > Smart AI > Edit AI persona…): tell the AI about you, such as your tone, pronouns, how to talk about your communication aid, and anything else it should know, so suggestions sound like you.

Settings

  • Your name and theme (colours).
  • Speech: choose the voice, pitch, speed and volume, and press Test voice to hear it.
  • Conversation and Smart AI: see above.
  • Data: import phrases from a text file, open or save a phrase set, or share it.

Your phrases are safe

  • Updating TexCom never changes or removes your phrases, categories or settings.
  • When an update brings new phrases, TexCom tells you once, and Settings > Data shows Add new TexCom phrases…. They go into a New phrases category for you to look through. Keep the ones you like and delete the rest.
  • To keep a copy, or move your phrases to another device, use Settings > Data > Save phrase set… and Open phrase set….
